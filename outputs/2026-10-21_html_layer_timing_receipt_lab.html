How to extend

Replace the simulated frame with an immersive session's requestAnimationFrame timestamp, log actual XRCompositionLayer values written in that callback, then associate a GPU completion / readability sample with the next visible compositor frame. A missing or late observation remains a receipt, not proof of presentation.
