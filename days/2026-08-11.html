注目事例と何が面白いかNotable example and why it matters

今日の注目は、WebXR Hit Test の結果をそのまま anchor 化せず、保存前に surface lock、pose stability、privacy trim、threshold の小さなファネルを通す考え方です。Hit Test explainer は real-world surface へ cursor を追従させる用途を説明し、MDN は `XRHitTestResult.getPose()` と `createAnchor()` の流れを示しています。ただし、どの候補を保存してよいか、どの候補は再スキャンに戻すかは体験設計側の判断です。そこを可視化すると、AR 配置 UI は単なる照準ではなく、運用に耐える保存ゲートになります。

Today's notable idea is filtering WebXR hit-test results through a small funnel before converting them into anchors: surface lock, pose stability, privacy trim, and threshold. The Hit Test explainer covers cursors that follow real-world surfaces, and MDN shows the path from `XRHitTestResult.getPose()` to `createAnchor()`. The application still has to decide which candidates are safe to save and which should return to rescan. Visualizing that decision turns AR placement UI from a cursor into an operational save gate.

表現・インタラクションのアイデアExpression and interaction idea

サンプルは viewer ray と 4 段の評価レーンを canvas に描き、ポインタ位置を照準として扱います。Surface noise、Motion drift、Privacy clamp、Anchor threshold を動かすと、候補が `anchor`、`rescan`、`trim`、`reject` に変わります。クリックまたは Freeze candidate で現在の候補を凍結し、Sample burst で複数候補をまとめて発生させます。Copy JSON は raw pose ではなく粗い判断ログだけをコピーするため、保存粒度の見直しにも使えます。

The sample draws a viewer ray and four evaluation lanes on canvas, treating pointer position as the targeting cursor. Changing Surface noise, Motion drift, Privacy clamp, and Anchor threshold moves a candidate between `anchor`, `rescan`, `trim`, and `reject`. Clicking or pressing Freeze candidate stores the current candidate, while Sample burst generates several candidates at once. Copy JSON copies only a coarse decision log instead of raw pose data, making saved-data scope visible.

制作に使えそうな技術メモProduction note

実装に落とす場合は、XR frame loop 内で `frame.getHitTestResults(hitTestSource)` を読み、最初の結果の `getPose(referenceSpace)` を使うだけでなく、数フレーム分の pose jitter、surface normal の変化、tracking confidence、ユーザーの照準滞在時間を合わせて anchor-ready score を作るのが扱いやすいです。保存時は `createAnchor()` に進む前に、プライバシー上保存してよい label と粗い pose band へ丸め、失敗時は raw camera や詳細 mesh に飛ばずに再スキャン指示へ戻す方が安全です。

In a real implementation, read `frame.getHitTestResults(hitTestSource)` inside the XR frame loop and use `getPose(referenceSpace)` from the first result, but also compute an anchor-readiness score from several frames of pose jitter, surface-normal changes, tracking confidence, and cursor dwell time. Before calling `createAnchor()`, quantize saved labels and coarse pose bands for privacy. On failure, return to a rescan prompt before escalating to raw camera or detailed mesh data.

Today's sample yields anchor-readiness score, surface lock, privacy trim, jitter meters, decision labels, accepted / rescan counts, preset, and a coarse JSON payload. Combined with product placement, exhibit labels, warehouse shelves, architecture review, or learning material, it can record the gap between the moment a user feels something is placed and the moment the system considers it safe to save. Voice guidance, haptics, AI quality checks, CMS placement history, and field tickets can make AR placement explainable after the session.
