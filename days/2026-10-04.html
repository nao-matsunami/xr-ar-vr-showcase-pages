注目事例と何が面白いかNotable example and why it matters

Chrome 144 の `XRVisibilityMaskChange` は、可視 viewport の mesh を vertices / indices として扱い、`XRView` の一意識別子で mask と view を対応付けやすくします。昨日は現在 mask と history mask の再投影 drift を見ました。今日はさらに半歩進めて、次の mask event が来る前に pose velocity、prediction lead、mask age から安全側の guard band を作ります。面白い点は、mask が `届いた後` の評価だけでなく、mask が `まだ届かない間` の描画判断も row 化できることです。

Chrome 144's `XRVisibilityMaskChange` represents the visible viewport mesh as vertices / indices and makes it easier to pair masks with views through unique `XRView` identifiers. Yesterday looked at reprojection drift between the current mask and a history mask. Today moves one half step further: before the next mask event arrives, it builds a conservative guard band from pose velocity, prediction lead, and mask age. The interesting part is turning not only post-arrival mask validation but also the in-between rendering decision into rows.

表現・インタラクションのアイデアExpression and interaction idea

本命サンプル `Predictive Visibility Mask Console` は view、mask age、prediction lead、pose velocity、acceleration を操作し、現在 mask、予測 mask、guard band、移動ベクトルを重ねます。派生サンプル `Prediction Miss Recovery Replayer` は、本命 row と実 mask miss の差分から、rollback full viewport、patch-and-hold、verify-mask-change、recover-tiled-rendering の流れを frame ごとに再生します。体験としては、XR 描画の省略を `攻める` よりも、予測が外れた時の戻り方まで含めて `失敗しても説明できる` 方向へ寄せています。

The main sample, `Predictive Visibility Mask Console`, lets you change view, mask age, prediction lead, pose velocity, and acceleration, then overlays the current mask, predicted mask, guard band, and motion vector. The derivative sample, `Prediction Miss Recovery Replayer`, uses the main row plus an actual mask-miss delta to replay rollback full viewport, patch-and-hold, verify-mask-change, and recover-tiled-rendering frame by frame. As an experience, it favors explainable recovery when prediction misses over aggressively reducing XR rendering.

制作に使えそうな技術メモProduction note

実装では texture や camera frame ではなく、`viewId`、maskAgeMs、predictionLeadMs、poseVelocityDeg、accelerationDeg、predictedShiftPx、guardBandPx、confidence、tileSavings、route だけを保存します。予測 confidence が高ければ predicted mask を submit、guardBand が広い場合は guard-band-expanded-mask、lead が長すぎる時は hold-until-mask-change、confidence が低い時は full-viewport-refresh に戻す、という小さな ladder にしました。実機では次の `XRVisibilityMaskChange` と突き合わせ、missPx を派生 replayer へ渡すと運用ログになります。

The implementation stores lightweight derived fields instead of textures or camera frames: `viewId`, maskAgeMs, predictionLeadMs, poseVelocityDeg, accelerationDeg, predictedShiftPx, guardBandPx, confidence, tileSavings, and route. The small ladder is: submit the predicted mask when confidence is high, expand the guard band when needed, hold until the next mask change when lead is too long, and return to full-viewport refresh when confidence is low. On device, compare the row with the next `XRVisibilityMaskChange` and send missPx into the derivative replayer for operational logging.

サンプルデータの可能性と組み合わせSample data potential and combinations

今日の row は、遠隔支援、展示 UI、XR 作業手順、酔いにくさ QA、WebXR パフォーマンス検証に使いやすいです。視界画像を保存せず、予測 shift、guard band、confidence、miss、rollback debt だけを残すため、セッション後に `なぜ full viewport に戻ったか`、`何フレームで tiled rendering に復帰したか` を説明できます。AI と組み合わせるなら、画像ではなく row の時系列を要約し、復帰遅延の主因を `mask age`、`pose velocity`、`prediction miss` へ分解させるのが相性よさそうです。

Today's rows fit remote assistance, installation UI, XR work instructions, comfort QA, and WebXR performance validation. Because they store predicted shift, guard band, confidence, miss, and rollback debt instead of view imagery, they can later explain why the app returned to full viewport and how many frames it took to recover tiled rendering. With AI, summarize row timelines rather than images and break recovery delay into mask age, pose velocity, and prediction miss.

俯瞰メモBird's-eye memo

Today moves from whether reprojection worked to predicting the next frame and recovering when it misses. Next, it is probably better to import multiple rows and rank causes than to add another isolated row type.
